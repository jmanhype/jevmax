# Ideal Customer Profile — AirPods Compatible Parts

Ideal customer: a DTC accessory buyer shopping for replacement AirPods parts.

They want a fast, inexpensive fix, not a new Apple device. They search when charging, fit, sound, or a lost part becomes urgent. Good customers are ready to buy today, understand these are compatible parts, and value same-day shipping.

Bad fits are researchers, students, people seeking free repairs, and shoppers expecting genuine Apple parts at generic-part prices. Always keep searches containing the seller's brand/product names.
