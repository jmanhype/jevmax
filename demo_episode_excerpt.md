# EPISODE 1 — draft excerpt (gate test)

INT. OBJECT 10-D SWITCHBOARD ROOM — NIGHT

Rows of signal-orange lamps. VALYA at the board, headset on, red pencil over
her right ear. She jacks a plug home with one precise motion.

A crackle. Her hand stops.

VALYA (whispering, in Russian, subtitled)
Lunar traffic is dead. Who is this?

STATIC. Then, faint, an American voice mid-mayday.

She grabs the logbook, writes fast — then freezes as the corridor door opens.
MAJOR KORABELNIKOV fills the frame. She covers the log with her forearm.

MAJOR KORABELNIKOV
Starshina. Your report.

VALYA
Routine lunar noise, Comrade Major.

He looks at her a long moment — at the red hair spilling loose over her
shoulders, at the lieutenant's insignia on her collar — and holds out his hand
for the logbook.
