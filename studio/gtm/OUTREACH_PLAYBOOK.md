# Founder outreach playbook

## Ideal client profile

### Best fit

- ecommerce founder or small operator
- already spending on Meta
- one hero product
- no research layer
- inconsistent creative testing
- limited production budget

### Poor fit

- wants guaranteed ROAS
- wants an ad autopilot
- has no product-market signal
- wants bulk AI content with no QA
- refuses read-only account access

## Positioning statement

> We do not sell AI videos. We sell an evidence-controlled ad test system:
> market signal, account audit, locked identity, ranked concepts, and eight
> controlled V6 videos before you scale spend.

## First-touch message

### Email or DM

```text
{{FIRST_NAME}}, I ran a bounded scan of the {{CATEGORY}} ad market and saw a
clear pattern: most visible competitors lean on direct product offers.

That means more AI videos will not fix the problem unless the hook and account
measurement are controlled first.

We run a Category Signal Sprint:
- bounded competitor scan
- read-only account audit
- ranked creative concepts
- eight controlled V6 ad tests
- QA and a top-two recommendation

It is $3,000 and takes 10 business days. Want me to send the one-page scope?
```

```text
{{FIRST_NAME}}, would a 15-minute call help more than another description?

I can walk through:
1. what your bounded category sample shows
2. what your ad account blocks
```

## Discovery questions

1. What product are you trying to sell most aggressively?
2. Where does traffic land today?
3. What are you currently spending on Meta?
4. What creative have you already tested?
5. What did the best performer actually do?
6. Does your pixel fire reliably?
7. Do you need Instagram placements?
8. Are there claims we cannot make?
9. Who approves ad spend?
10. What would make this test successful?

## Objection handling

### “AI content looks generic.”

We do not start with generation. We start with market signal, a locked
identity, and specific visual grammar. The AI render is the last controlled
step, not the strategy.

### “Why not just make 50 videos?”

Because unranked volume increases noise. We score concepts first, then render
eight controlled V6 variants and QA every output.

### “Can you guarantee results?”

No. We guarantee evidence, controlled production, measured cost, QA, and a
top-two recommendation. Performance claims require an approved live test.

### “Why not use the best video model?”

Our default is V6 because it measured 40 credits per test and preserved
identity, product, composition, and no-text constraints. Premium models can
cost 150 or 850 credits per video and may drift more.

### “Why do you need account access?”

Read-only access lets us find broken measurement and placement issues before
creative spend. We do not change live ads without explicit approval.

## Qualification exit

Say:

```text
This is not a fit yet. Fix {{BLOCKER}}, then come back for the sprint.
```

Typical blockers:

- no product-market signal
- no destination page
- no measurement events
- expectation of guaranteed performance
