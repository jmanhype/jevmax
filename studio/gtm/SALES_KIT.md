## What the client receives

### 1. Category Signal Audit

- bounded Meta Ad Library sample
- current ad IDs and advertiser patterns
- hook and offer classification where available
- current versus prior snapshot diff
- explicit bounded-sample limitations

### 2. Account Audit

- campaign status
- pixel or dataset activity
- Instagram linkage
- lead-gen readiness
- budget exposure
- three prioritized fixes

### 3. Brand-Safe Identity

- fictional character system, or reuse of an approved client identity
- no real-person likeness
- product-handling rules
- AI-disclosure guidance

### 4. Creative Test Sprint

- 8–12 market-informed concepts
- Lost Future style-family directions
- Eyecandy visual-technique mapping
- concept gate before rendering
- eight V6 720p five-second vertical videos
- prompt manifest
- render log
- QA report

### 5. Launch Recommendation

- top two candidates
- risk notes
- landing-page checklist
- measurement plan
- explicit client approval checklist
