# Category Signal Audit

## Client outcome

The client learns what competitors are running in a bounded category sample,
what is broken in the ad account, and which three actions come first.

## What is included

- Bounded Meta Ad Library scan
- Ad IDs and delivery start dates
- Hook and offer mix for tagged ads
- Top advertiser summary
- Current-versus-prior sample diff
- Daily or weekly snapshot baseline
- Read-only account audit
- Pixel, page, Instagram, and campaign checks
- Three prioritized actions
- Raw JSON and report retention

## What is not included

- Whole-library census
- Guaranteed performance
- Campaign changes
- Unapproved tagging of every new ad
- Survival claims before a 60-day-old baseline exists

## Timeline

3–5 business days.

$750–$1,000.
