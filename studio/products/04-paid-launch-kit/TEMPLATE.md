## 9. Measurement plan

| Question | Metric | Source | Threshold before scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Did the hook stop scroll? | 3s video play or thumbnail CTR | platform | client-defined |
| Did the promise interest the buyer? | CTR | platform | client-defined |
| Did the page continue the promise? | landing view or conversion event | platform | client-defined |

Do not report ROAS lift without a defined control, spend window, and measured
baseline.

## 10. Approval gate

The client must explicitly approve:

- final asset
- final copy
- destination URL
- budget
- audience
- campaign objective
- disclosure setting
- measurement interpretation

Jevmax Studio does not silently launch or modify live ads.
