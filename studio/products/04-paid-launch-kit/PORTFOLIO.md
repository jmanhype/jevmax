# Paid Launch Kit

## Client outcome

The client receives a launch-ready package: selected hero creative, thumbnail,
copy variants, CTA, AI-disclosure instructions, measurement plan, and an
explicit approval checklist.

## What is included

- Hero video
- Static opener/thumbnail
- Backup asset recommendation
- Three primary-text variants
- Three headlines
- Three hooks
- CTA recommendation
- Meta AI-disclosure instructions
- Landing-page promise checklist
- Measurement plan
- Launch approval checklist

## What is not included

- Silent campaign launch
- Unauthorized live ad changes
- Guaranteed ROAS
- Copy or claims without documented support
- Platform-compliance legal advice

## Timeline

2–3 business days after a Creative Test Sprint has a selected candidate.

## Price guidance

$750–$1,500 as an add-on.

## Normal next product

Growth Loop.

The launch kit creates the baseline assets and measurement plan; the Growth
Loop watches performance, competitive changes, fatigue, and the next test.
