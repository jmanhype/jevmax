# Growth Loop portfolio summary

## Client outcome

Growth Loop installs a governed weekly and monthly operating rhythm for an
advertising category. The client gets bounded competitive scans, preserved ID
snapshots, honest sample differences, read-only account checks, explicit refresh
gates, and a documented route into the next creative test. It replaces anecdotal
monitoring with dated evidence and stops unapproved automation at the account
boundary.

## Evidence highlight

- Verified 103-ID baseline snapshot with SHA-256 integrity record.
- Active Monday 08:00 top-50 read-only scan automation.
- Current bounded difference: 21 retained, 29 current-only, and 53 absent IDs.
- First valid 60-day survival read: **2026-11-19**.
- Offline regression: **36 / 36 passed**.
- Package assembly cost: **0 generation credits** and **0 live ad changes**.

## What is included

- Weekly bounded scan and difference runbook.
- Append-only unique-ID snapshot rules and integrity checks.
- Monthly survival-eligibility and review cadence.
- Read-only account readiness audit checklist.
- Cost, token, QA, disclosure, and approval gates.
- Refresh handoff into Creative Test Sprint.

## What is not included

- Whole-library census or share-of-voice claims.
- Guaranteed ROAS, production lift, or blind holdout results.
- Survival claims before a comparable snapshot is at least 60 days newer.
- Automatic ad changes, paid deployment, or campaign management.
- Unapproved premium generation.

## Routing

Normal next product: **Creative Test Sprint**.

If the category definition or query plan changes, route first to **Category
Signal Audit**. If the monthly review produces an approved launch plan, route to
**Paid Launch Kit**; live changes still require explicit client approval outside
this loop.
