# Creative Test Sprint

## Client outcome

The client receives controlled, ranked creative variants and a recommendation
of at most two candidates for paid testing.

## What is included

- 8–12 market-informed concepts
- Lost Future style-family mapping
- Eyecandy technique mapping
- Jev concept gate
- Up to eight image boards
- Approved identity/product reference
- Eight V6 720p five-second videos
- Prompt manifest
- Render log
- QA report
- Measured credit report
- Top-two winner recommendation

```text
GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst board
V6 720p 5-second no-audio video
```
