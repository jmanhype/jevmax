# Technique ideas — Eyecandy → Ava campaign

Source: https://eyecannndy.com/ (free visual-technique library; GIF examples per
technique). Mapping created 2026-09-20 while building persona-ava.json.
Convention: each variant gets a stable id (V001, V002…) and, once concepts are
gated by `score_briefs.py`, a brief_id (see persona-ava.json `variant` block).

## Stills — promptable now (edit the v2 schema sections noted)

| Variant | Eyecandy technique | Change in schema | Idea |
|---|---|---|---|

| V007 | Central Framing | symmetric composition, subject dead-center | Clean product-hero framing |

## Video grammar — for when the campaign goes Reels/Story

Whip Pan, Match Cut, Speed Ramp, Freeze Frame, Slow Motion, Handheld, Camera
Roll (the "camera-roll dump" format — a proven UGC ad pattern). Browse the
technique pages for reference GIFs before writing shot lists.

## Notes

- ~2/3 of Eyecandy is motion grammar; stills can only use compositional and
  lighting language (Hard Light, Central Framing, Silhouette, Reflections…).
- Taxonomy observation: Eyecandy is essentially the "format" axis of workflow
  #1 (hook/format tagging) formalized as a browsable library.
