# Lost Futures — 100 Additional Account Niches

These are a separate additional-niche namespace, not extensions of the original 186 master-board IDs. Here IDs `001–100` refer only to this list.

## Exclusion brief

These candidates deliberately exclude retro anime, mecha girls, genopunk, cyberpunk OVA, the prior twenty account lanes, generic VHS creepiness, public-access conspiracy TV, local consumer investigations, dot-com webcasts, cable-news crises, emergency VHS, home shopping, CD-ROM history, courtroom evidence, department-store robots, civic kiosks, smart homes, airport people-movers, maritime radar, and the already-mined anime registers.
