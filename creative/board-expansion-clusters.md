## Business-concept expansion clusters

### Cluster 5 — Conversion proof engines

Turn attention into belief before the click.

- **Claim Lab**: one product claim per creative, with visual proof, source
  framing, and objection handling.
- **Comparison System**: brand-versus-alternative comparisons without unfair
  disparagement.
- **Review Story Arcs**: reviews converted into recurring customer narratives.
- **Objection Killers**: repeatable formats for price, fit, compatibility,
  trust, and setup doubts.

Jevmax route: Category Signal Audit supplies the evidence; Paid Launch Kit owns
claim review and measurement.

### Cluster 6 — Commerce lifecycle systems

Cover every commercial stage, not only cold traffic.

- **Onboarding Unboxings**: first-use, setup, and day-one success sequences.
- **Replenishment Triggers**: replacement, refill, upgrade, and wear-out reminders.
- **Bundle Architects**: visual systems for kits, bundles, and cross-sell offers.
- **Win-Back Worlds**: lapsed-customer, trade-in, and return-reason narratives.

Jevmax route: Creative Test Sprint selects the concept; Growth Loop schedules
the lifecycle refresh.

### Cluster 7 — Community participation

Use the audience as production capacity under brand constraints.

- **Remix Kits**: brand-safe prompts, templates, and asset packs.
- **Challenge Engine**: recurring challenges with stable rules and product constraints.
- **Fan Casting**: community scenarios selected through a judgment gate.
- **Ambassador Storylines**: recurring personas and episodic product interactions.

Jevmax route: Creative Test Sprint tests the format; Growth Loop governs cadence.

### Cluster 8 — Vertical studio products

Package repeatable studios for specific buyer types.

- **Local Service Studio**: regional service areas, before/after proof, and local register.
- **Ecommerce Hero Studio**: one product across hooks, objections, formats, and seasons.
- **B2B Case Story Studio**: customer proof, buyer drama, and implementation narrative.
- **App Activation Studio**: feature demos, onboarding moments, and habit sequences.

Jevmax route: extend the five-product portfolio only after a dogfood run and
validator update.

### Cluster 9 — Cultural register factories

Sell distinctive visual language as the product.

- **Lost Futures Ad Time Capsules**: product ads rendered as dead regional/media formats.
- **Regional Commercial Registers**: local TV, public access, retail, dealership,
  and civic broadcast styles.
- **Fictional Sponsor Universes**: recurring fake brands, sponsors, and broadcast IDs.
- **Era-Mix Product Myths**: one product reimagined through incompatible media eras.

Jevmax route: Brand Identity System owns the register; Creative Test Sprint tests
commercial applications.

### Cluster 10 — Measurement and production ops

Productize the control layer.

- **Creative Telemetry Dashboard**: hook, format, spend, fatigue, and survival views.
- **Fatigue Radar**: early warning for frequency and CTR decay.
- **Asset Version Control**: seed, persona, variant, prompt, and render lineage.
- **Moderation Preflight**: lean-prompt, identity-reference, and retry checks.

Jevmax route: Growth Loop and the deterministic validators; no paid generation
by default.

## Cinema/story expansion clusters

### S5 — Audio-first worlds

- **Fictional Podcast Networks**: show identities, episode art, sonic signatures.
- **Radio Drama Revivals**: serialized audio drama with visual companion frames.
- **ASMR Narratives**: whispered or textured storytelling around sound.
- **Sonic Franchise IDs**: recurring audio logos and theme systems.

### C6 — Archive and documentary

- **Fake Oral Histories**: fictional experts recounting impossible events.
- **Museum Exhibits**: interactive displays for invented artifacts.
- **Archive Restoration Stories**: fictional media archaeology and recovered formats.
- **Nature-doc Microseries**: stylized species, ecosystems, or product ecosystems.

### E7 — Interactive and game cinema

- **Branching Video Stories**: audience-choice narrative trees.
- **Playable Ad Worlds**: short game-like product simulations.
- **ARG Fragments**: clues, artifacts, websites, and broadcast intrusions.
- **Game-Trailer-Style Product Myths**: epic reveal grammar for ordinary objects.

### W8 — Comedy and satire

- **Workplace Mockumentaries**: deadpan office ecosystems.
- **Fake Institutional Broadcasts**: corporate, civic, or public-access parody.
- **Sitcom Micro-episodes**: recurring cast, tiny conflict, product complication.
- **Ad-Industry Satire**: commercials about making commercials.

### L9 — Simulated live formats

- **Fake Breaking Broadcasts**: staged continuity and ticker aesthetics without
  real-event deception.
- **Countdown Events**: episodic anticipation and reveal formats.
- **Live Shopping Fictions**: host, demo, urgency, and audience reaction.
- **Broadcast Intrusions**: stylized fictional interruptions for mystery universes.

### X10 — Commerce/IP crossover

- **Product Mythology**: origin stories, fake patents, and design lineages.
- **Shoppable Story Worlds**: narrative scenes where products are plot objects.
- **Franchise Merch Lines**: fictional show branding turned product system.
- **Collector Universes**: rarity, variants, lore, and display culture.
