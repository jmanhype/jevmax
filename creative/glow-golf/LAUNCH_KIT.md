## 2 · Visual constitution (the law)

Everything posted must obey all four locks. A render that breaks one is not
posted — it's re-prompted.

1. **Palette lock — UV blacklight only.** Hot magenta, electric cyan, acid
   green, deep violet-black. Illumination comes FROM glow paint and blacklight
   tubes, never from daylight or white studio light.
2. **Era lock — 1990s family entertainment center.** Hand-painted plywood
   cutout props, green carpet turf, PVC pipe rails, glow-in-the-dark paint,
   string lights on open ceilings, worn rental putters. Nothing modern-LED,
   nothing digitally clean.
3. **Texture lock — analog always.** Every image carries film grain, camera-
   flash falloff, camcorder scanlines, or photolab color shift. A crisp render
   is a failed render. (This also hides model tells.)
4. **Subject freedom — subjects roam, grammar doesn't.** Holes, scorecards,
   course maps, signage, ball's-eye POVs, maintenance artifacts, staff notice
   boards. The windmill appears when natural — a signature fans learn to spot,
   never a mandatory element. (Corrected model per retroosunset analysis:
   anchor lives in the avatar, unity in the grammar, freedom in the subjects.)

**The anchor:** the PFP is a designed glowing-windmill mark (spec §3). It never
changes and never appears as a feed crop.

## 3 · PFP spec

Full-bleed 1:1. Single windmill silhouette, UV palette on a dark field, glow
paint bleed, subtle film grain. All critical detail inside the center-safe
80% circle (IG crops circular + story ring). Must read at 110px: one shape,
two colors minimum contrast, no text, no fine linework.

## 7 · Growth mechanics (from the @retroosunset field study)

North-star metric: **engagement rate** (likes+comments+saves ÷ reach), never
follower count — the reference account proved follower counts can be hollow
(91.7K followers, double-digit likes).

- **Comment ring:** spend 15 min/day leaving genuine, specific comments on
  small AI-nostalgia/analog-horror accounts (the anime__art__alchemist-type
  scene). Reciprocity is the cold-start engine at this scale.
- **Reels 2–3/week** for reach; stills hold the grid identity.
- **Collab card held in reserve:** when account #2 (A068 Discontinued
  Wallpaper Samples) launches, use IG collab posts to cross-seed both feeds —
  the one mechanic the reference account runs well.
- **30-post checkpoint (go/no-go on account #2):** any post ≥3% engagement
  rate, saves trending upward, comment ring ≥2 dozen mutual accounts. If
  missed, fix captions/reels before adding accounts.

## 9 · Production runbook

Model `gpt-image-2.5-flare`, quality `1080p`, 0 credits (Pro promo; ~30/day
ceiling observed 2026-09-21 — if hangs/500044s appear, stop and resume next
pool window). Lean prompts: era+format first, one noun-phrase subject, no
appearance inventories, no skin language, no destruction words. Prompts of
record: `PROMPTS.md` in this directory. Log: `glowgolf_log.csv`. Idempotency
scheme: `jevmax-gg-<slug>-a<n>`, fresh key per attempt. Downloads via
`pixverse asset download --dest`. Status-9 zombie tasks get asset-deleted
before retrying (slots are finite).

**Rendering law additions (learned 2026-09-22, now mandatory clauses):**
1. Era camera/camcorder formats ALWAYS spawn period datestamps unless the
   prompt says "no datestamp, no signs, no lettering anywhere."
2. Number grids get miscounted (an 18-hole card rendered 19 rows) — either
   forbid legible numerals ("faint indistinct smudges") or composite real
   numbers in post.
3. Every finished asset passes the visual QA gate (constitution compliance,
   palette lock, no readable text) before it counts as slate-ready.
