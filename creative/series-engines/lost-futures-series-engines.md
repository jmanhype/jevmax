# Lost Futures — Series Engines (narrative account cluster)

The 100-niche fleet is artifact-still engines. This cluster is the narrative
lane: accounts whose **post unit is an episode or sequence**, not an artifact.
Sourced from the 186-item master board's cinema/story material (fake trailers,
micro-fiction, title sequences, broadcast fragments, universe bibles, the
Uncverse). Ownable originals only — no direct-IP lanes.

Readiness tags (agent judgment, not Jev-scored):
- **READY** — runs on the current stack today (stills + single-move i2v reels, text lives in captions)
- **PREVIZ** — multi-shot sequences; unlocks when Shot Composer enters the pipeline
- **COMPOSITE** — needs the text-overlay compositing step (same gate as A069/A067)

## The ten lanes

### N01 — The Trailer House
A fictional regional studio's trailer archive: 20–40s fake trailers for films that never existed, rotating genre + era (each era gets its own grain and grade). **Engine:** genre × era grid means the catalog never repeats. Video-native flagship of the cluster. [PREVIZ]

### N02 — One-Minute Archives
60-second analog-horror episodes, one location each, single slow camera move, story told by what's in the room. **Engine:** every episode is a new room; the constraint is the brand. The cheapest lane to produce — closest to Glow Golf's production shape. [READY]

### N03 — Micro-Fiction Serial
Carousel stories: 4–6 cards, one still per beat, prose in the caption (never in the render — text-in-caption is stack-native). **Engine:** serialized characters and running hooks; cliffhanger cadence. [READY]

### N04 — The Title Sequence Museum
Opening credits for shows that never existed. Typography-forward — which means real type composited over generated backplates, making it the most "designed" lane. **Engine:** one show = one identity system; the museum framing lets styles roam while curation holds the brand. [COMPOSITE]

### N05 — Spec Commercial Vault
15s spots for impossible products. The Ava pipeline already proved this exact shape end-to-end (product → identity → variant batch) — this is that machinery pointed at a fictional network instead of one client product. **Engine:** infinite product catalog, format locked. [READY]

### N06 — Broadcast Interrupted
Emergency-broadcast fragments, cable-crisis lower-thirds, sign-off loops, dead-air with something moving in it. Static-heavy, audio-driven, single-move friendly. **Engine:** the interruption format; every post ends mid-sentence. [READY]

### N07 — The Universe Bible
World-bible pages as carousel series — the Hi8 character-bible page system from the master board: model sheets, location plates, prop studies, continuity notes (deco text only, real notes in captions). **Engine:** one original universe per "volume"; the strongest identity-system play in the fleet — it can also feed satellite accounts. [READY]

### N08 — The Documentary Bureau
Deadpan 45–60s docs about impossible subjects (a town that rents its weather; the wallpaper museum; the glow golf course — bureau episodes can visit fleet worlds, making it the shared-universe narrator). Interview stills, b-roll reels, authoritative captions. **Engine:** subject pipeline = the whole Lost Futures universe is its episode list. [READY — strongest strategic pick]

### N09 — Uncverse
The flagship original show itself (S2E01 master prompt exists). Episode stills + scene reels + bible drops. The only lane with a pre-existing audience artifact. **Engine:** the show's season structure. [PREVIZ for scene reels; stills READY]

### N10 — Night Desk Monologues
Recurring original characters delivering 15-second desk monologues (the unhinged-character cluster). Illustrated personas (moderation-safe, consistency via frozen seeds — the Ava method applied to a cast). **Engine:** rotating cast of 4–5 locked personas; catchphrases become the account's memes. [READY]

## Cluster rules

1. Same fleet law: anchor PFP per account, locked grammar, roaming subjects.
2. Text lives in captions or compositing — never in raw renders.
3. Max **one** narrative account launches at a time; they are heavier per post than artifact accounts (story debt is real). Recommended first: **N02 or N05** (cheapest engines), with **N08** as the strategic play once 2+ fleet accounts exist — it narrates the whole universe.
4. This cluster activates Shot Composer (N01, N04, N09 scenes) and the compositing step (N04) — those dependencies are features, not blockers: the READY lanes run today.
