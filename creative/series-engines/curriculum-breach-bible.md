## The machine

- **Institution:** Unified Lesson District, 1991
- **Rule:** If you teach Slide 13, Room 13 appears. If you skip it, the district
  sends a substitute who is not human.
- **Protagonists:** Ms. Kline (compliance teacher), two students who keep
  noticing extra slides
- **Cost:** Correcting the lesson erases what the class learned yesterday
- **Loop:** routine → rule violation → institutional response → unsettling consequence
- **Look:** overhead projector, acetate transparencies, wood-grain CRT, PA system
- **Lengths that work:** 15s / 25s / 45s — no VFX monster, just classroom
