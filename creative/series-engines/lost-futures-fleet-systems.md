engines and N01–N10 are narrative engines, this cluster is the **fleet's
operating system**: engagement mechanics, the hub account, character
satellites, sonic lanes, and artifact-niche expansion. IDs F01–F12.

## Engagement engines

Pristine 90s stock-photo scenes with one impossible detail hiding in plain
sight. Caption: "Find it." Comment fuel IS the format — followers answer,
argue, tag friends. **The strongest engagement mechanic available to an image
account; nothing in the 100 niches has it.** [READY]

### F02 — Caption Court

swatch, next exhibit. Every post ends with a poll hook. [READY]
