# EP001 THE VOICE ON 14 MEGACYCLES

## EP001-SC001 内 · OBJECT 10-D 主机房 · 深夜

Rows of signal-orange lamps in the dark. VALYA at the board, headset on, red
pencil over her right ear. She jacks a plug home with one precise motion.

[画面文字] ОБЪЕКТ 10-Д · СМЕНА 3 · 23:47

The board: ЛУННЫЙ ТРАФИК — НОЛЬ. Dead air hiss.

[SFX] A crackle. Her hand stops mid-motion. Lean-in.

Static parts. An American voice, mid-mayday, ragged:

SEVEN-THREE [VO, in English, subtitled]
— negative on hatch, repeat, negative — is anybody copy — frequency check —

Valya's eyes go to the frequency counter: 14.

## EP001-SC002 内 · 主机房 · 紧接

Her hand drifts to the red cut-key. Hovers.

SEVEN-THREE [VO, English collapsing into slow, learned Russian]
Скажи им. Я ещё здесь.
(Tell them. I am still here.)

The hand leaves the cut-key. Above it, a brass placard: СЕКЦИЯ 7 —
НЕЗАРЕГИСТРИРОВАННЫЙ ПЕРЕХВАТ = ИЗМЕНА. (SECTION 7 — UNLOGGED INTERCEPT
= TREASON.)

Valya takes the red pencil down from her ear.

## EP001-SC003 内 · 主机房 · 一分钟后

She writes in the logbook: date. Frequency 14. Voice, male, American.
Не опознано. Her pencil presses hard enough to dent the page.

[SFX] The corridor door. MAJOR KORABELNIKOV fills it. He doesn't enter
far — he never does. He waits.

KORABELNIKOV：Ночной журнал, старшина.
(Your night log, Starshina.)

Her forearm slides over the page. Smooth. Practiced.

VALYA：Обычный лунный шум, товарищ майор.
(Routine lunar noise, Comrade Major.)

KORABELNIKOV（не двигаясь）：Значит, тихая смена.
(A quiet shift, then.)

He holds out his hand. She gives him the logbook.

## EP001-SC004 内 · 主机房 · 紧接

He leafs. Stops. Turns the book toward the lamp.

HER PAGE. The entry is gone. Not torn — lifted. A fresh ink smudge where
the words were, still tacky under the lamp light.

[连续性] 红铅笔仍在瓦利娅手中；日志纸面有新鲜墨痕但无字迹。

Valya's face: nothing. Perfect parade rest. Inside — the floor is gone.

KORABELNIKOV（закрывая журнал, тихо）：Интересная ночь, старшина.
(Interesting night, Starshina.)

He sets the logbook down between them and walks out. The door closes.

[SFX] Under the hiss: lamp 14 flickers. Twice. Like a knock.

Valya looks at the pencil in her hand — the only proof the voice existed.

[转场] 黑场。灯 14 的橙色余辉。
