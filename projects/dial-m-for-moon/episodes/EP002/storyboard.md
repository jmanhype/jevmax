# EP002 — "THE MISSING PAGE" · 3-shot pipeline test

**Source beat:** The log page didn't vanish — it was taken: her own supervisor
lifted it on Moscow's orders and burned it in the stove.
**Cliffhanger image:** The supervisor found dead at his desk. Headphones still
on. The board lamp for 14 lit.

**Look (all shots):** 1959 16mm Kodak Ektachrome reversal film, slight organic
grain, halation on practicals only, mild archive vignette. Vertical 9:16.
1959 Soviet period throughout. No on-screen text overlays.

**camera:** CU over her shoulder onto the logbook. Her fingers find the torn

**motion:** fingers trace the torn edge, stop on the smudge; her gaze drops
**world:** log canvas cover edge, warm lamp glow, night switchboard bay soft behind
**audio:** paper whisper, her breath catching, room hum
**ends on:** her fingers on the torn stub; eyes downcast at the gap
