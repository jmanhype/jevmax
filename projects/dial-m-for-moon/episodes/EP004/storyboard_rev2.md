# EP004 — "TWO TAPES" (Revision 2)

Revision 2 rebuilds the episode around a single directing doctrine (below).
Same story, same shots, same constraints — but every shot now directs what
Valya **feels**, not just what her hands do. DRAFT for Jay's approval; if
approved, this replaces storyboard.md.

## Directing doctrine

**Primary lens: Hitchcock.** The audience knows the danger before she
finishes taking it (copying the tape is treason — the bomb is under the
table from S02 on). Suspense comes from watching her risk it, not from
mystery. Tools: look → see → reaction grammar; reaction shots over action
shots; object close-ups as emotional carriers (reels, pencil, crate lid,
headset = the ticking clock); silence and ordinary detail over noise.

**Borrowed, sparingly:**
- *Polanski* — the warm headset stays ambiguous. Someone was here… or is
  she breaking? Never answered.
- *Kurosawa* — S07 holds stillness. One held shot of Valya listening does
  more than three cuts.

**Value map:** every beat moves her from safer to less safe. If a beat
doesn't tighten the screw, it doesn't belong (this is why S03 stays cut).

**Framing arc — the vise:** the episode starts medium-wide in the bay and
tightens to close-ups as her safety shrinks. By S09/S10 there is nowhere
left for the camera — or her — to go.

**Caption voice:** everything she "says" is her private voice — things a
scared person tells herself (defiance, self-calming), never explanations
for the audience. Where the face can carry it, there is no caption.

**Standing constraints (unchanged):** one visible character; the unseen
operator exists only as the warm headset; struck-through/redacted name,
never an invented cosmonaut; hard cuts default, match-on-action only for
hand-carried objects; S07→S08 deliberately unconditioned (time gap); tape
hiss bridges picture ruptures; title card ≤3–4s; red pencil anchors the
labeling; final face held longer than comfortable; bay stays quiet (reel
mechanics, pencil sounds, silence around the headset reveal); locked-off
cameras; strict continuity gates.

## Beats (9 shots + title card, ~103s)

### S01 — title card (edit graphic, 3s over black)
DIAL M FOR MOON — "TWO TAPES". Bullet-hole/print-worn series mark.
(Unchanged.)

### S02a — the coordinates (DONE — winner.mp4, keep)
She plots the mayday coordinates — and stills, pencil hovering.
*Feeling:* professional certainty curdling into dread. The numbers point
at a dead man.
*Value:* curiosity → dread.
Caption: "These are not American coordinates."

### S02b — the redacted file (DONE — s02b_winner.mp4, keep)
She pulls the flight file. The cosmonaut's name is struck through. The
flight never happened.
*Feeling:* the chill of understanding. The state erased a man — and now
she knows.
*Value:* suspicion → certainty. She knows too much to walk away.
Caption: "He never flew."

### S03 — CUT (unchanged)
Stays cut. A faceless hardware insert with no value turn.

### S04 — the crime
She threads the second deck and starts the dub. **This is the treason —
direct it like one.** Her hands work fast and quiet; she keeps stopping
to listen; her eyes cut toward the door at the sound of the reels. The
ordinary (threading tape) is charged because we know the stakes.
*Feeling:* fear, held under control. She's trying not to shake.
*Value:* safe → criminal. The act is done; it can't be undone.
*Animation note (keyframes A/B locked):* direct micro-performance, not
just motion — a hesitation before she presses in, a pause mid-threading
to listen, hands that want to hurry but force themselves steady.
Caption: "One copy. For me." (defiance — she's choosing this)
Ends: the copy reel full, her hand lifting it off the spindle.

### S05 — her handwriting on the evidence
Beat 1: the red pencil labels the copy box — in her handwriting. Her
hand **hesitates mid-word**; she almost doesn't write it. Then she does.
Beat 2: her face, close — she looks at what she's written. The cost
lands. (Look → see → reaction: the label, her writing, her fear.)
*Feeling:* the moment the crime gets her name on it.
*Value:* criminal → exposed. The evidence now points at her.
No caption — the face carries it.
Ends: box closed, taped. (Prop continuity into S06.)

### S06 — the hiding place
Archive room. She nests the labeled box among the spare headsets in the
headband crate, closes the lid. Her shoulders drop. One breath.
*Feeling:* relief — fragile, the kind you don't trust.
*Value:* exposed → hidden. The danger is buried. (This relief is what
S08 takes away — it has to read.)
Caption: "No one looks here." (self-calming — she's reassuring herself)

### S07 — the pause (Kurosawa stillness)
Beat 1: she stands at the crate, hand still on the lid — and goes
**still**. Listens. Turns her head toward the door.
Beat 2: hold on her face, listening. Nothing happens. The silence
stretches longer than comfortable. Then she moves — light off, leaves.
Door clicks.
*Feeling:* the animal sense that something is wrong, with no proof.
*Value:* hidden → watched? (the question, not the answer)
No caption. Silence is the beat.

### S08 — the empty crate (time passage, hard cut in, unconditioned)
Later. Lamp light different. She opens the crate — her hand searches
among the headsets. Nothing. Beat 2: her hand stills inside the crate.
Then her face: the hiding spot is compromised.
*Feeling:* violation. The one safe place wasn't safe.
*Value:* relief → violation.
Caption: "Gone."

### S09 — the warm headset (Polanski ambiguity)
Beat 1: close on the rack. Her hand moves along the earcups, stops at
one, rests inside it. Warm.
Beat 2: her face — **is someone else here, or is she breaking?** Hold.
Do not answer it.
*Feeling:* the floor dropping out. Someone was just listening.
*Value:* violation → presence.
No caption. Silence around the reveal. (Warm = recently worn; identified
by her touch, never by a visual change.)

### S10 — the hook
Beat 1: her face, close, lamplight — her eyes move from the rack toward
the door. (The look.)
Beat 2: hold her face. The question hangs. Longer than comfortable.
(The audience completes the empty doorway themselves — their
imagination fills the threat, which is stronger than showing it.)
*Feeling:* dread with no object. She doesn't know what she's afraid of
yet — but we do.
*Value:* presence → unknown. The episode ends open.
No caption.
