# EP004 — "TWO TAPES"

First episode built under the format notes (johnny-the-gunslinger-study,
silence-ad-study). Canon: the mayday's coordinates match a cosmonaut who
never flew; Valya makes a private copy of the tape and hides it in the
headband crate. Cliffhanger: the hiding spot is empty — and a second
headset on the rack is still warm.

DRAFT for Jay's directorial approval — no shots generated yet.

## Format rules applied

- Title card opens the episode (edit graphic, consistent placement).
- ~5s beats, one location/action per beat; the cut is the continuity reset.
- Object inserts as the visual spine: tape reels, the labeled copy, the
  headband crate, the headset rack (rigid, repeatable — our "watch").
- Wardrobe lock: full Valya anchor list in every shot prompt.
- Captioned dialogue discipline: short decisive captioned lines, no
  lip-sync reliance. Tape audio is hiss + the mayday fragment, never
  mouths.
- One character on screen (Valya). The other operator is a presence —
  a warm headset — never seen.
- Ends on the open hook. Resolve the beat (the copy is made and hidden),
  not the story (it is gone, and someone was just listening).

## Beats (10 shots x 10s, two 5s beats each)

### S01 — title card (edit graphic, 3s over black, then:)
DIAL M FOR MOON — "TWO TAPES". Bullet-hole/print-worn series mark,
consistent with the per-episode title-card rule.

### S02 — the coordinates
Night switchboard bay. Valya at the board, headset on, the mayday tape
playing on the deck beside her. She plots the coordinates on a chart,
then stills — pencil hovering. Caption: "These are not American
coordinates." Beat 2: she pulls the flight file; the cosmonaut's name is
struck through. The flight never happened. Caption: "He never flew."

### S03 — object insert: the reels
Close on the reel-to-reel deck: both reels turning, tape hissing.
Her hand enters frame and stops the reels. (Spine insert — hold 5s,
then the hand.)

### S04 — the copy
She threads a second deck and starts the dub: fingers on reels, tape
pathing through the heads, both decks rolling. Caption: "One copy. For
me." Ends: the copy reel full, her hand lifting it off the spindle.

### S05 — object insert: the label
Close on her hands: the red pencil (over-ear anchor in use) labels the
copy reel's box in her handwriting. We do not need to read it; the act
of labeling is the beat. Ends: box closed, taped.

### S06 — the hiding place
Archive/store room. The headband crate — a wooden crate of spare
Bakelite headsets — sits on a shelf. She opens it, nests the labeled box
among the headsets, closes the lid. Caption: "No one looks here."

### S07 — the pause
She stands at the crate, hand still on the lid — and turns her head
toward the door. Nothing there. Beat 2: she leaves, switching the room
light off. Door clicks.

### S08 — the empty crate (time passage, hard cut in)
Later. Same room, lamp light different. She opens the crate — her hand
searches among the headsets. Nothing. Beat 2: her hand stills inside the
crate. Caption: "Gone."

### S09 — object insert: the rack
Close on the headset rack beside the crate. Her hand moves along the
earcups, stops at one, rests inside it. Beat 2: she holds still. The
implication lands without a word. (Warm = recently worn. Someone was
just listening.)

### S10 — the hook
Her face, close, lamplight. She looks from the rack toward the door.
No caption. The episode ends on her look — the story still moving.
(Running time ~103s with the title card.)

## Character anchors (every shot prompt)
Valentina "Valya" Orlova, 27. Ash-blonde low bun, dark-olive ribbon,
sky-blue signals piping on the tunic, Bakelite headset (on head in bay
scenes; around neck or on rack in archive scenes — note which), red
pencil over right ear, plug cord looped around left wrist when at the
board. 1959 16mm Kodak Ektachrome, vertical 9:16, period-true, no
modern sci-fi.

## Wardrobe/prop lock notes
- The tape box label is in her handwriting; keep the box visually
  identical S05 -> S06 (prop continuity across the hard cut).
- The headband crate: same wooden crate, same shelf position S06/S08.
- The headset rack: same rack both visits; the "warm" earcup is
  identified by her touch, not by any visual change.
