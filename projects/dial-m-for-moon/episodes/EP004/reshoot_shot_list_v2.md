# EP004 v2 reshoot shot list — VIKTHOR camera grammar

Status: BUILT 2026-09-24, awaiting Jay's greenlight to generate.
Rule: native/in-house pipeline only. No Kling/PixVerse. No second face.
Reference grammar: the bathhouse video — camera always moving (slow pan/push),
inserts as punctuation, micro-performance, light locked from one side.

## New standing lock (adopted from the reference breakdown)
- **Lamp-left rule:** key light comes from frame left for the whole episode.
  Every prompt in this list states it. (This also kills the S02→S03
  brass/green lamp jump class of error going forward.)

## R-S05 — the crime, lateral drift (replaces S05 winner)
- Start state: over her shoulder, dub deck center frame, her right arm
  running visibly shoulder→hand on the dub control. Head in profile,
  eyes toward the door. Brass lamp light from frame left.
- Motion target: her hand eases the control forward; reels turn; her
  eyes flick door→deck→door. Fear held under control, trying not to shake.
- Camera: slow lateral drift left→right across the deck, ~30° of arc,
  10s. The move is the scene's tension — the bathhouse pan grammar.
- Cause/effect: hand moves control → reels turn → she reacts to the
  sound they make, glancing at the door.
- Continuity anchors: red pencil over right ear, headset around neck,
  sky-blue piping, bun; deck with NO badge (Soviet machine).
- Performance: micro — a held breath, a glance. No big acting.
- Sound: deck hum low under hiss (already in the v1 design; keep).
- Copy-prompt: "1959 Soviet night listening post, Ektachrome 16mm still,
  vertical 9:16, photoreal. Over the shoulder of a 27-year-old woman
  signals operator (ash-blonde low bun, dark-olive ribbon, olive tunic,
  sky-blue piping, Bakelite headset around neck, red pencil over right
  ear): her arm runs shoulder to hand on a reel-to-reel dub control,
  reels turning. Her head in profile, eyes glancing toward a door in
  background. Warm brass desk lamp key light from frame left. Camera
  drifts slowly left to right across the deck for the whole shot.
  Her hand eases the control forward; she glances door, deck, door,
  afraid of the sound. No text, no captions, no second person."

## R-S07 — the pause, slow push-in (replaces S07 winner)
- Start state: ECU/medium close. Headset on. Utterly still, eyes
  unfocused mid-distance, breath held, listening. Brass lamp left.
- Motion target: nothing in her moves except her eyes, which narrow
  almost imperceptibly — the animal sense something is wrong, no proof.
- Camera: slow push-in, ~15% over 10s. Stillness inside a moving frame.
- Cause/effect: no external cause; the push-in IS the pressure.
- Continuity anchors: headset on head (not around neck), red pencil
  over right ear, bun, piping; near-silence.
- Performance: micro — breath held, a 2mm eye shift. Kurosawa stillness
  kept; the camera supplies the motion the reference demands.
- Sound: near-silence, faint room tone only (v1 design; keep).
- Copy-prompt: "Photoreal ECU, 1959 Ektachrome, vertical 9:16. A
  27-year-old Soviet woman signals operator (ash-blonde low bun,
  Bakelite headset ON head, red pencil over right ear) utterly still,
  eyes unfocused at mid-distance, breath held, listening. Warm brass
  lamp key from frame left, face half in shadow. Camera pushes in
  very slowly for the whole shot. Her eyes narrow almost
  imperceptibly. Nothing else moves. No text, no captions."

## R-S10 — the warm headset, arc to the earcup (replaces S10 winner)
- Start state: Bakelite headset resting on the desk, her hand hovering
  just above the earcup, not touching. Her troubled face behind the hand.
- Motion target: her hand lowers a fraction closer, feeling for warmth
  in the air; her eyes stay on the earcup, then lift toward the door.
- Camera: slow arc (~25°) across the tabletop toward the earcup, 10s,
  ending closer on the earcup with her face soft behind it.
- Cause/effect: warmth identified by her hovering hand and her face —
  never by a visual change to the headset (Polanski ambiguity kept).
- Continuity anchors: headset on desk (not on her), red pencil over
  right ear, bun; TOTAL SILENCE per v1 design.
- Performance: micro — the hover, the troubled eyes, the lift toward
  the door at the end.
- Copy-prompt: "Photoreal close-up, 1959 Ektachrome, vertical 9:16.
  A Bakelite headset rests on a wooden desk; a woman's hand hovers
  just above one earcup, not touching. Behind the hand, the troubled
  face of a 27-year-old Soviet signals operator (ash-blonde low bun,
  red pencil over right ear, olive tunic, sky-blue piping). Warm brass
  lamp key from frame left. Camera arcs slowly across the tabletop
  toward the earcup. Her hand lowers a fraction; her eyes stay on the
  earcup, then lift toward the door. Total silence. No text, no
  captions, no second person."

## INS-S02 — the pencil stops (new insert, cut into S02)
- Start state: ECU of the red pencil tip mid-scratch on the chart,
  her fingers gripping it, chart lines visible.
- Motion target: the scratching STOPS DEAD mid-stroke. A beat of
  stillness. (The realization lands here; her face in S02 carries it.)
- Camera: locked ECU with a barely-perceptible drift; the stop is the event.
- Cause/effect: her eyes find the coordinates → hand obeys before
  thought → pencil halts.
- Continuity anchors: red pencil (same one), chart paper, her fingers;
  lamp-left warmth.
- Sound: pencil-scratch hiss STOPS DEAD with the picture (v1 design).
- Copy-prompt: "Photoreal extreme close-up, 1959 Ektachrome, vertical
  9:16. A red pencil tip scratching across a chart, a woman's fingers
  gripping it. Warm brass lamp light from frame left. Mid-stroke the
  pencil STOPS DEAD and holds still. No text legible, no captions."

## INS-S10b — the earcup (new insert, punctuation for R-S10)
- Start state: extreme close-up of the Bakelite earcup on the desk,
  her fingertips entering frame edge, hovering.
- Motion target: fingertips hover, tremble once, withdraw a millimeter —
  the decision not to touch.
- Camera: locked ECU. The tremble is the event (reference: the eye opening).
- Cause/effect: perceived warmth → approach → fear → withdrawal.
- Continuity anchors: Bakelite texture, her fingertips, lamp-left warmth.
- Sound: total silence.
- Copy-prompt: "Photoreal extreme close-up, 1959 Ektachrome, vertical
  9:16. A Bakelite headset earcup on a wooden desk; a woman's
  fingertips enter frame and hover above it, tremble once, withdraw a
  millimeter. Warm brass lamp light from frame left. No text, no captions."
