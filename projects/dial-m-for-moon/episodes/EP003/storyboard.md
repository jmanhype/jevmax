# EP003 — "THE CONNECTION"

Continuity test: one character (Valya), one setting (the night switchboard
bay), one continuous action across three shots. The whole episode is a single
patch-cord connection, cut on the movement.

## S01 — the signal
Valya at the switchboard, headset on. A lamp blinks; she hears the incoming
signal, reaches up, and pulls a patch cord from its socket.
Ends: hand pulling the cord free, cord in her hand.

## S02 — the patch (match on action from S01's tail frame)
Her hand carries the cord across the board and seats it in a new socket.
Lamps respond along the row.
Ends: plug seated, her fingers releasing it.

## S03 — the log (match on action from S02's tail frame)
She turns from the board to the desk, takes the red pencil from over her ear,
and writes the entry in the logbook.
Ends: pencil moving across the page.

## Character anchors (all three shots)
Valentina "Valya" Orlova, 27. Ash-blonde low bun, dark-olive ribbon,
sky-blue signals piping on the tunic, Bakelite headset, red pencil over
right ear, plug cord looped around left wrist.
1959 16mm Kodak Ektachrome, vertical 9:16, period-true, no modern sci-fi.
