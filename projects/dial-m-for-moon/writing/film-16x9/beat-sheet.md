# DIAL M FOR MOON — beat sheet (16:9 short film, ~12 min)

Written for **Hook A ("The Call")** — the writer's pick, pending the rank gate. If the gate advances B or C, this sheet is rewritten for the winner.

Canon: Valentina "Valya" Orlova, 27, starshina, Soviet Signal Troops, night-shift switchboard supervisor at the Object 10-D lunar listening post, 1959. Ash-blonde bun + olive ribbon, sky-blue collar piping, Bakelite headset, red pencil over right ear, plug cord coiled on left wrist. Left-eyebrow scar: script-only canon (referenced in prose, never required visually).

1. **Night. Routine.** Valya works the switchboard alone — plugs, logbook, tungsten lamps. The ritual of a woman who is good at her job. (Setup)
2. **The dead jack glows.** No line assigned. Signal-orange, steady, wrong. (Inciting incident)
3. **She checks the roster.** Nothing. Procedure says report it. Her thumb finds the thin scar through her left eyebrow — the tell she only shows when she's about to lie. (Debate)
4. **She plugs in.** Lifts the Bakelite headset from around her neck to her ears. (Decision — point of no return)
5. **Static. Then: "Valentina Orlova."** A flat voice, Russian with no accent. (Hook lands)
6. **"Do not answer."** The jack goes dark. (Reversal — the warning IS the hook)
7. **Morning. Volkov.** The security chief questions her about an "unauthorized patch" — someone logged it. Nadia won't meet her eyes. (Pressure)
8. **The logbook.** She finds the entry — in her own handwriting — recording a shift she never worked. Dated tomorrow. (Escalation — the uncanny turns personal)
9. **Night again.** The jack glows. Nadia sees it too — and looks away. (Ally burns; Valya is alone)
10. **"Who is this?"** Valya plugs the dead jack and speaks into it. (Climax choice — she disobeys the warning)
11. **Her own voice answers.** Older. Tired. "Don't hang up. They're listening." (Climax reversal)
12. **Every lamp on the board dies.** Except one. It pulses like a heartbeat. Valya looks up — through the skylight, the Moon. TITLE. (Button image)

## Production notes
- One set (switchboard room) + one corridor for the Volkov scene. Valya Element-anchored; Volkov and Nadia are the only other principals.
- The uncanny stays in sound design: static, the half-tone hum drop, the heartbeat lamp. No sci-fi visuals.
- Estimated render budget (post-gate): ~8–10 shots × 60 cr.
