# DIAL M FOR MOON — three competing opening hooks (16:9 short film)

Three mechanically different openings for the rank gate (hook / era / engine). The gate advances one; the beat sheet is written for the writer's pick, Hook A, pending the gate's verdict. Machine-readable version: `opening-hooks.csv`.

## Hook A — "The Call" (in media res, intimate)
Open on Valya alone at the night switchboard. A dead jack glows; she answers; a voice speaks her name: "Valentina Orlova. Do not answer." Continues the already-rendered shot_01 beat (calm focus → plug insert → crackle → sharp lean-in) directly into story. Performance-driven, cheapest to produce, the mystery is personal from frame one.

## Hook B — "The Inspection" (institutional)
Open in daylight: a general inspects Object 10-D; everything is pristine and false. During the tour, Valya's board shows one unassigned blip — and instead of reporting it, she pockets the log page. Her first small defiance. The thriller is the institution, not the mystery; the engine is "how long can she hide what she knows."

## Hook C — "Two Empires" (dual perspective)
Cross-cut from frame one: Valya at Object 10-D and an American operator at a U.S. Army listening station, both hearing the same impossible signal on the same night. The tagline made literal — ONE FREQUENCY. TWO EMPIRES. The film becomes a race: who answers first, and what does answering cost.
