# DIAL M FOR MOON — vertical short-drama story engine (9:16)

## The engine in one line
Every night shift, the board gives Valya one call that doesn't exist. Logging it is treason; ignoring it is worse.

## Pressure → response → counter-pressure
- **Pressure arrives as:** a dead jack glows signal-orange — a call from no line, on no assigned frequency. It names her, counts down, or plays back something only she should know.
- **Valya's standard response:** log nothing, investigate alone. She plugs in because not knowing is worse than the risk.
- **Counter-pressure:** Major Volkov, the post's security chief, hunts "unauthorized monitoring." Post rule: unlogged signals don't exist — reporting one is confessing curiosity, and curiosity is treason-adjacent. Nadia, the next-desk operator, may be his informant. May not be.

## Why she can't exit
Object 10-D is a closed posting; requesting transfer reads as guilt. And the signal knows her name now — it's personal. Walking away doesn't end it.

## What changes each round (never just louder)
Each episode must change at least two of three: what she knows about the signal / how much Volkov suspects / one irreversible cost (a falsified log page, a burned ally, a missed check-in that gets noticed).

## Characters: goals, stakes, bottom lines
- **Valya** (27, starshina, night-shift switchboard supervisor): wants to understand what she's hearing. Bottom line: she won't feed her operators to Volkov.
- **Major Volkov** (security chief): wants the anomaly contained — buried or weaponized, his call. Bottom line: Object 10-D's true purpose stays secret.
- **Nadia** (operator, next desk): wants to survive the posting unnoticed. Bottom line: herself.

## What ends the engine
She answers fully and learns what Object 10-D really listens to — or Volkov catches her mid-listen and she must choose between the post and the truth.

## Boundaries (visual / sound / scale)
Single set: the switchboard room (corridor for pressure scenes). Night shifts. Tungsten amber vs signal-orange lamps. 1959 hardware only. The uncanny lives in **sound** — never in sci-fi visuals. Valya is Element-anchored; no new principals beyond Volkov and Nadia.
