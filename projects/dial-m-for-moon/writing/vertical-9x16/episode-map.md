# DIAL M FOR MOON — vertical episode candidates (9:16)

Four candidates for the episodes gate (hook / cliffhanger / producibility). The gate advances the top 2–3 to the shot budget. Machine-readable version: `episode-map.csv`.

## EP1 — "Dead Jack"
**Beat:** Night shift at the Object 10-D switchboard. Valya works the board; a jack with no assigned line glows signal-orange. Procedure says report it. She plugs in instead. Static — then a voice speaks her full name.
**Cliffhanger:** "Valentina Orlova. Do not answer." The jack goes dark.
**Cost:** Cheapest — one set, Valya + Nadia, Element-anchored.

## EP2 — "Tomorrow's Log"
**Beat:** She hunts the logbook for the call. No record exists. Volkov questions her about unauthorized monitoring. In the log she finds her own handwriting recording a shift she never worked.
**Cliffhanger:** The entry is dated tomorrow.
**Cost:** Cheapest — one set, Valya + Volkov.

## EP3 — "Nadia"
**Beat:** Nadia admits she heard it too — then denies it to Volkov. He orders Valya to sign a statement: nothing happened. As she lifts the pen, the dead jack glows, and the voice speaks Volkov's name aloud in the room.
**Cliffhanger:** Volkov goes pale. He heard it too.
**Cost:** Cheap — one set, three principals, no new assets.

## EP4 — "The Reply"
**Beat:** Alone on night shift, Valya decides: she will answer. She plugs the dead jack and speaks into it: "Who is this?" Silence. Then her own voice, older, tired: "Don't hang up. They're listening."
**Cliffhanger:** Every lamp on the board dies. Except one. It pulses like a heartbeat.
**Cost:** Cheapest — one set, Valya alone, sound design carries it.
