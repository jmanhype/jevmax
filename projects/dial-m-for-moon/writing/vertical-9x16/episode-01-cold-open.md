# EP001 — "Dead Jack" (cold open)

Vertical 9:16 · ~2 min · Single set: switchboard room, Object 10-D lunar listening post, night.
Canon: Valentina "Valya" Orlova, 27, starshina, Soviet Signal Troops, night-shift supervisor.

## EP001-SC001 INT. SWITCHBOARD ROOM, OBJECT 10-D — NIGHT

Tungsten lamps. Rows of jacks glow signal-orange. VALENTINA "VALYA" ORLOVA (27)
works the board with quick precise hands. Ash-blonde hair in a tight bun, olive
ribbon. Sky-blue piping at her collar. The black Bakelite headset hangs around her
neck like a collar. A plug cord is coiled around her left wrist. Red pencil over
her right ear.

[SFX] Switchboard clicks. Low room hum. A chair scrapes — NADIA (20s), next desk,
half-asleep over her own board.

VALYA
(Without looking up)
Log the Minsk trunk before you dream, Nadia.

NADIA
It's logged. You're the one still awake.

Valya plugs a cord. A beat of routine. Then — one jack glows. No line is assigned
to it. The lamp burns signal-orange, steady, wrong.

Valya stares. Her thumb finds the thin scar through the tail of her left eyebrow —
an old habit when she's deciding whether to lie.

[SFX] The room hum drops half a tone.

NADIA
Valya? That jack isn't on the roster.

VALYA
I know.

She looks at the logbook. Then at the glowing jack. Then — she plugs in.

She lifts the headset from around her neck to her ears.

[SFX] Static. A long breath of it. Then, through the crackle, a VOICE — flat,
unhurried, Russian with no accent at all:

VOICE [OS]
Valentina Orlova.

Valya goes still.

VOICE [OS]
Do not answer.

The jack goes dark.

Nadia is staring at her.

NADIA
What was that?

Valya pulls the headset down around her neck. Her hand is steady. Her thumb finds
the scar again.

VALYA
Nothing. Wrong patch.

She turns back to the board. The camera holds on her face — she is lying, and she
knows Nadia knows.

[END OF COLD OPEN]
