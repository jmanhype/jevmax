# Cross-Page Consistency Rules (Learned 2026-05-19)

When building character bibles across multiple pages, the following rules must be followed:

## Mandatory Consistency Check
After generating each new page, explicitly verify against Page 1 (and prior pages):

- Helmet / headgear style and shape (most common source of drift)
- Suit design, patches, logos, and hardware placement
- Overall proportions and body type
- Color palette and material treatment
- Signature props and accessories

## QC Honesty Rule
Never claim "consistent" or give high scores without verifying the specific details above. Overstating consistency triggers user frustration. When in doubt, regenerate the page.

## Historical / Non-Anime Adaptation
When creating bibles for non-anime aesthetics (e.g. 1960s NASA film, 1970s Giallo, 1980s British TV), the same 8-page structure still applies, but the prompts must be rewritten to match the target film stock, lighting, grain, and production design rather than anime style.

## Anti-Patterns Observed
- Assuming visual consistency without explicit comparison
- Accepting lower film texture quality on later pages
- Letting helmet/prop details drift between pages
